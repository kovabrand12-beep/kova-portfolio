About Verde Goods

Verde Goods started as a weekend plant stand and grew into a small online shop shipping houseplants that are chosen specifically for people who forget to water on schedule.

We work directly with a handful of growers rather than a distribution warehouse, which means fewer plants sitting in trucks and more that actually make it to your windowsill in good shape.

Grown, not warehoused

Every plant ships direct from partner growers, not a distribution center.

30-day guarantee

If it doesn't make it, we replace it — no questions asked.

Care guides included

Every order comes with a plain-language care card, not a QR code.

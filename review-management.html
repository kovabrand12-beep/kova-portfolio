How we do it

Real reviews only

Reviews only help if they are real. These are the rules we work to, and they are the same ones Google sets.

  • Every customer is asked. Not only the ones we expect to be happy. Picking and choosing is against Google's rules and it shows.
  • No rewards for reviews. No discounts, no gift cards, no prize draws. We never buy a review and never write one.
  • Only your own customers. Requests go to people you have actually done work for, and every message tells them how to opt out.
  • No ranking promises. More recent reviews help people choose you. Nobody can promise you a position on Google, and we will not.
